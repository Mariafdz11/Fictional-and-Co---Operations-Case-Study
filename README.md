# Fictional & Co — SME Operations & Finance Transformation Case Study

A practical portfolio project showing how a growing multi-site SME could improve financial visibility, cost control, operational processes and systems before further expansion.

## Business Scenario

Fictional & Co is a fictional four-site hospitality business based in London.

- **Annual revenue:** £4.8m
- **Employees:** 65
- **Active suppliers:** 45
- **Sites:** Soho, Vauxhall, Borough and Camden
- **Growth stage:** Preparing for a potential fifth location

The business has grown successfully, but many finance and operational processes remain fragmented, manual or difficult to scale.

## Project Objective

Assess the current finance, operations and systems environment and develop practical improvements across:

- Business Operations
- Finance Operations
- Cost Control
- Supplier Management
- Management Reporting
- KPI Design
- Process Improvement
- Systems & SaaS
- Operational Controls
- Implementation Planning

## My Role

**Operations Transformation Manager**

## Approach

The project follows a structured improvement process:

1. Document the current operating model
2. Assess operational, financial and systems risks
3. Define the management KPI framework
4. Analyse financial and operational data
5. Build a management dashboard
6. Identify material exceptions
7. Redesign key workflows
8. Review systems, SaaS and automation opportunities
9. Build a 90-day implementation roadmap

## Key Findings

The diagnostic identified several priority areas:

- POS data is not efficiently connected to financial reporting
- there is no central purchase-order process
- supplier invoices arrive through multiple channels
- supplier pricing, terms and contract information are inconsistently maintained
- cash-flow forecasting relies heavily on manual updates
- labour performance is reviewed too retrospectively
- management reporting depends on multiple systems and spreadsheets
- committed spend is not consistently visible before invoices arrive

The analysis also identified **Vauxhall** as the priority site for review, with weaker revenue performance, greater labour pressure and elevated supplier and maintenance costs.

## Selected Analysis Findings

### Vauxhall

Vauxhall emerged as the clearest priority site.

Key findings included:

- revenue approximately **4.3% below budget**
- higher COGS pressure
- recurring labour overspend
- agency labour costs
- lower site contribution than the other locations
- elevated maintenance spend

### Supplier Cost Control

The analysis identified:

- Fresh Foods spend at Vauxhall approximately **6.6% above baseline**
- Rapid Repairs spend at Vauxhall approximately **27.4% above baseline**
- later-period beverage spend drift at Camden

### Positive Comparators

Borough remained broadly stable and Camden showed stronger revenue momentum.

This supported an **exception-based management approach** rather than applying the same corrective actions to every site.

## KPI Framework

The management framework focuses on:

- Revenue vs Budget
- Gross Margin %
- Cost of Goods Sold %
- Labour Cost %
- Site Contribution
- Supplier Spend Variance
- Budget vs Actual Operating Costs
- 13-Week Cash Forecast
- Accounts Payable Ageing
- Contract / Renewal Exposure

KPIs are split between **weekly operational review** and **monthly financial review**.

## Core Recommendation

Fictional & Co does not require a full technology replacement programme.

The strongest opportunity is to improve processes, ownership and management visibility around the systems already in place, then introduce targeted automation where it provides clear operational value.
