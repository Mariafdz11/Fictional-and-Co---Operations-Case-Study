# Fictional & Co — 90-Day Implementation Roadmap

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager

## Purpose

This roadmap translates the diagnostic, KPI framework, process redesign and systems review into a practical 90-day implementation plan.

The objective is to improve control, visibility and scalability before Fictional & Co progresses with Site 5.

---

## Roadmap Principles

The implementation plan follows five principles:

1. **Fix foundations first** — standardise processes and data before automating them.
2. **Prioritise material issues** — focus first on the areas with the greatest impact on cost, control and visibility.
3. **Avoid unnecessary disruption** — retain systems that are already fit for purpose.
4. **Assign clear ownership** — every initiative requires a named owner and target date.
5. **Measure progress** — improvements should be reviewed through agreed KPIs and management actions.

---

# Days 1–30 — Stabilise and Standardise

## 1. Supplier Invoice Process

**Actions**
- Introduce a dedicated invoice inbox.
- Notify suppliers of the new submission process.
- Define invoice approval ownership by site and cost category.
- Standardise required invoice information.
- Create an exception log for missing approvals, duplicates and coding issues.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Finance & Admin Assistant / Site Managers

**Expected Outcome**
A single, traceable invoice intake and approval process.

---

## 2. Supplier Register

**Actions**
- Consolidate the existing supplier spreadsheet.
- Add:
  - supplier owner,
  - category,
  - agreed pricing,
  - payment terms,
  - contract dates,
  - notice periods,
  - renewal dates,
  - approved status.
- Identify incomplete or high-risk supplier records.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Support**
Finance Manager

**Expected Outcome**
A reliable central supplier record for commercial and operational use.

---

## 3. Contract Register

**Actions**
- Consolidate contract information into one register.
- Identify contracts due for renewal within 90 days.
- Add ownership and notice periods.
- Introduce reminder dates.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Improved visibility of upcoming renewals and commercial commitments.

---

## 4. KPI Ownership and Definitions

**Actions**
- Confirm final KPI definitions.
- Assign a data source and owner to each KPI.
- Confirm weekly and monthly reporting cadence.
- Define material exception thresholds.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Operations Manager

**Expected Outcome**
A consistent reporting framework with clear ownership.

---

## 5. Vauxhall Priority Review

**Actions**
- Review:
  - revenue performance,
  - labour costs,
  - agency dependency,
  - COGS,
  - Fresh Foods spend,
  - maintenance spend.
- Identify immediate corrective actions.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Support**
Finance Manager / Vauxhall Site Manager

**Expected Outcome**
A focused action plan for the highest-risk site.

---

# Days 31–60 — Strengthen Controls and Management Visibility

## 6. Purchasing Approval Process

**Actions**
- Define delegated purchasing authority.
- Agree approval thresholds.
- Introduce a lightweight purchase request / PO process for material spend.
- Define escalation rules for:
  - non-budgeted spend,
  - unusual purchases,
  - contractual commitments.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Support**
Finance Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Earlier visibility of committed spend and stronger purchasing control.

---

## 7. Weekly Management Review

**Actions**
- Launch weekly review of:
  - revenue vs budget,
  - labour cost %,
  - COGS %,
  - cash visibility,
  - accounts payable ageing,
  - material operational exceptions.
- Record actions, owners and due dates.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Support**
Finance Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Earlier intervention before issues become month-end problems.

---

## 8. Management Dashboard

**Actions**
- Standardise dashboard inputs.
- Confirm site-level reporting structure.
- Add exception-based status indicators.
- Review performance side by side across all sites.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Operations Manager

**Expected Outcome**
A repeatable management view combining financial and operational performance.

---

## 9. Labour Control

**Actions**
- Introduce weekly labour-to-sales review by site.
- Compare scheduled vs actual labour.
- Track overtime and agency use.
- Require explanation for material variances.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Support**
Site Managers

**Expected Outcome**
Improved in-month labour visibility and faster corrective action.

---

## 10. Supplier Cost Monitoring

**Actions**
- Introduce monthly supplier variance reporting.
- Monitor:
  - spend vs baseline,
  - unit-price movement where available,
  - supplier concentration,
  - material exceptions.
- Prioritise renegotiation or review where required.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Operations Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Earlier identification of supplier cost pressure.

---

# Days 61–90 — Automate and Prepare to Scale

## 11. Invoice Workflow Automation

**Actions**
- Evaluate invoice capture / approval workflow options.
- Test:
  - OCR,
  - duplicate detection,
  - approval routing,
  - site / cost centre coding,
  - Xero integration.
- Pilot with selected suppliers or one site before full rollout.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Operations Transformation Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Reduced manual invoice handling and improved auditability.

---

## 12. Contract and Renewal Automation

**Actions**
- Introduce automatic reminders for:
  - 90 days,
  - 60 days,
  - 30 days before renewal.
- Assign renewal ownership.
- Track status and commercial outcome.

**Owner**
Operations Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Fewer missed renewal opportunities and stronger negotiation lead time.

---

## 13. Reporting Automation

**Actions**
- Identify the highest-effort manual reporting steps.
- Standardise exports and data structures.
- Explore:
  - scheduled imports,
  - API connections,
  - automated spreadsheet refresh,
  - lightweight BI where justified.
- Automate only stable, repeatable processes.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Support**
Operations Transformation Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Shorter reporting cycle and reduced manual consolidation.

---

## 14. Cash-Flow Process Improvement

**Actions**
- Standardise 13-week cash-flow inputs.
- Define recurring categories:
  - opening cash,
  - expected receipts,
  - payroll,
  - VAT,
  - supplier payments,
  - rent,
  - major commitments.
- Assign update ownership and weekly review cadence.

**Owner**
Finance Manager

**Expected Outcome**
Improved forward visibility of liquidity and upcoming cash pressure.

---

## 15. Site 5 Readiness Review

**Actions**
- Review whether the new operating model is functioning consistently.
- Confirm:
  - invoice process adoption,
  - supplier register completeness,
  - purchasing controls,
  - KPI reporting,
  - labour monitoring,
  - contract visibility,
  - cash-flow process.
- Identify any remaining gaps before expansion.

**Owner**
Managing Director

**Support**
Finance Manager / Operations Manager / Operations Transformation Manager

**Expected Outcome**
A clear go / no-go readiness assessment for operational scalability.

# Roadmap Summary

| Period | Focus | Main Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Stabilise and standardise | Invoice process, supplier register, contract register, KPI ownership, Vauxhall review |
| Days 31–60 | Strengthen controls and visibility | Purchasing approvals, weekly review, dashboard, labour monitoring, supplier variance reporting |
| Days 61–90 | Automate and prepare to scale | Invoice automation, renewal reminders, reporting automation, cash-flow improvement, Site 5 readiness review |

---

## Key Dependencies

The roadmap depends on:

- management agreement on ownership and approval thresholds,
- reliable supplier and contract data,
- site manager adoption,
- stable KPI definitions,
- consistent source data.

Automation should not proceed until the relevant process is standardised.

---

## Success Measures

Progress should be reviewed using:

- % of invoices received through the central channel,
- invoice approval cycle time,
- % of material purchases approved before commitment,
- supplier record completeness,
- number of overdue contract reviews,
- weekly labour variance,
- reporting cycle time,
- number of overdue management actions,
- number of material supplier exceptions identified before month-end.

---

## Expected 90-Day Outcome

At the end of 90 days, Fictional & Co should have:

- clearer financial and operational ownership,
- stronger purchasing and supplier controls,
- faster management visibility,
- improved labour and cost monitoring,
- more consistent processes across sites,
- reduced reliance on manual administration,
- and a more scalable operating model for Site 5.
