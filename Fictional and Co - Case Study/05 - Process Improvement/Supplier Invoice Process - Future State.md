# Fictional & Co — Supplier Invoice Process: Future State

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager  
**Process:** Supplier Invoice Management

## Purpose

This document defines the proposed future-state supplier invoice process for Fictional & Co.

The redesigned process aims to improve control, consistency, visibility and scalability while retaining Xero as the core accounting platform.

---

## Future-State Workflow

1. Supplier sends invoice to a dedicated central invoice email address.
2. Invoice is automatically or manually captured into the invoice-processing workflow.
3. Invoice is checked against:
   - supplier record,
   - site / cost centre,
   - purchase order or approved spend where applicable,
   - agreed payment terms.
4. Site Manager or budget owner confirms receipt of goods or services.
5. Invoice is approved through a defined approval workflow.
6. Finance reviews coding, VAT treatment and accounting completeness.
7. Approved invoice is posted into Xero.
8. Invoice is included in the scheduled payment run.
9. Exceptions are flagged for follow-up rather than handled informally.

## Proposed Process Improvements

### 1. Dedicated invoice inbox

All suppliers are instructed to send invoices to one central address.

**Expected benefit:**
- Single source of truth
- Fewer missed invoices
- Easier monitoring and reconciliation
- Cleaner audit trail

### 2. Defined approval workflow

Each invoice is routed to a named budget owner or site manager for approval.

**Expected benefit:**
- Clear accountability
- Traceable approvals
- Reduced payment delays
- Easier escalation of exceptions

### 3. Supplier and site coding at intake

Invoices are consistently associated with the correct supplier, site and cost category.

**Expected benefit:**
- Better reporting quality
- Faster month-end review
- Improved site-level cost visibility

### 4. PO / approved-spend matching

Where a purchase order or pre-approved commitment exists, the invoice is matched against it.

**Expected benefit:**
- Better control over unexpected spend
- Earlier identification of pricing or quantity differences
- Stronger link between purchasing and finance

### 5. Exception-based review

Invoices with missing approvals, unusual pricing, duplicate references or other issues are flagged.

**Expected benefit:**
- Finance can focus on exceptions instead of manually checking every invoice in the same way
- Faster processing of routine invoices
- Stronger control over irregular items

### 6. Structured payment runs

Approved invoices are included in scheduled payment runs according to payment terms and cash-flow priorities.

**Expected benefit:**
- Better cash planning
- Fewer ad hoc payments
- More consistent supplier management

---

## Systems Approach

The recommendation is not to replace Xero.

The preferred approach is to improve the process around Xero through:

- centralised invoice intake,
- standard approval rules,
- clearer supplier data,
- and, where appropriate, invoice-capture or approval automation.

Any SaaS solution should be selected based on business requirements rather than introduced solely for automation.

---

## Expected Impact

The redesigned process should provide:

- fewer missing or duplicated invoices,
- stronger approval controls,
- improved supplier and site-level visibility,
- reduced administrative chasing,
- faster month-end processing,
- and a process that can scale more effectively to Site 5.

---

## Success Measures

The future-state process can be monitored using:

- % of invoices received through the central channel
- average invoice approval time
- number of overdue approvals
- number of duplicate / exception invoices
- number of invoices without valid supplier or site coding
- percentage of invoices matched to approved spend or PO where applicable
