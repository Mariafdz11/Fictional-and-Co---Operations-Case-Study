# Fictional & Co — Management Reporting Process: Current State

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager  
**Process:** Management Reporting

## Purpose

This document maps the current management reporting process at Fictional & Co before redesign.

The objective is to understand how management information is assembled, where manual work occurs, and how quickly decision-makers receive useful financial and operational insight.

---

## Current-State Workflow

1. Sales data is exported from the POS system.
2. Financial data is exported from Xero.
3. Budget information is taken from spreadsheets.
4. Additional operational information is gathered from separate files.
5. Finance manually consolidates the information.
6. Monthly P&L and sales summaries are prepared.
7. Management commentary is added.
8. The reporting pack is circulated to management.
9. Issues are discussed after the reporting period has largely closed.

## Pain Points Identified

### 1. Multiple source systems

Management reporting depends on several different systems and spreadsheets.

**Risk / impact:**
- Time spent gathering and reconciling data
- Greater risk of version-control issues
- More manual dependency

### 2. Manual consolidation

Finance combines information from Xero, POS and spreadsheets manually.

**Risk / impact:**
- Reporting takes longer to prepare
- Repetitive administrative work
- Increased risk of manual errors

### 3. Reporting is mainly retrospective

Management receives formal reporting monthly.

**Risk / impact:**
- Cost or trading issues may only be identified after they have already affected results
- Less opportunity to intervene during the month

### 4. Operational KPIs are not consistently embedded

The current pack focuses mainly on P&L and sales reporting.

**Risk / impact:**
- Labour, supplier spend, committed costs and operational exceptions may not receive enough attention
- Financial results may be reviewed without understanding the operational drivers behind them

### 5. Limited exception-based reporting

Management receives information, but there is no consistent structure for highlighting only material variances requiring action.

**Risk / impact:**
- Important issues can get lost in detailed reporting
- Management time may be spent reviewing information that does not require intervention

---

## Current-State Assessment

**Priority:** High

The current reporting process provides useful financial information but depends heavily on manual consolidation and retrospective review.

As Fictional & Co grows, the reporting model needs to become more standardised, timely and action-oriented.

---

## Improvement Objective

Create a reporting process that:

- reduces manual consolidation,
- provides earlier visibility,
- combines financial and operational KPIs,
- highlights exceptions,
- and links management information directly to actions and ownership.
