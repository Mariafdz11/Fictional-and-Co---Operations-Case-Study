# Fictional & Co — Operations Diagnostic Framework

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager

## Purpose

This diagnostic reviews Fictional & Co's current finance, operational and systems environment to identify inefficiencies, control gaps and scalability risks before further expansion.

The assessment focuses on:

- Finance Operations
- Cost Control
- Supplier Management
- Reporting & KPIs
- Processes & Workflows
- Systems & SaaS
- Controls & Ownership
- Scalability

## Priority Ratings

**High** — Material issue affecting control, visibility, efficiency or scalability.  
**Medium** — Inefficient or inconsistent process that should be improved.  
**Low** — Current process is broadly effective and requires limited change.

---

## Diagnostic Assessment

| Area | Current State | Finding | Business Impact | Priority | Recommended Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales & Finance Integration | POS reporting is separate from financial reporting | Sales data requires manual consolidation with finance data | Slower reporting and increased manual work | High | Improve integration or standardise automated data transfer |
| Purchasing | Site managers order directly from approved suppliers | No central purchase-order process | Limited visibility of commitments and weaker cost control | High | Introduce a proportionate purchase approval / PO process |
| Supplier Invoices | Invoices arrive through several channels | No standardised invoice intake process | Risk of missed, duplicated or delayed invoices | High | Introduce a dedicated invoice channel and standard approval workflow |
| Supplier Management | Supplier data is stored in a basic spreadsheet | Pricing, terms, ownership and contract information are incomplete | Reduced commercial visibility and weaker supplier control | High | Create a central supplier register with defined ownership |
| Cash Flow | Forecast maintained manually in Google Sheets | Significant manual input required to keep forecast current | Time-consuming and risk of outdated information | Medium | Standardise forecast inputs and explore data automation |
| Management Reporting | Reporting depends on Xero, POS and several spreadsheets | Reporting process is fragmented and manual | Slow production and retrospective decision-making | High | Define a standard reporting model and streamline source data |
| Labour | Labour cost mainly reviewed through monthly reporting | Limited in-month visibility of labour performance | Cost issues may be identified too late | High | Introduce weekly labour monitoring against sales and budget |
| Contracts | Contracts stored in Drive with partial spreadsheet tracking | Renewal dates are not consistently controlled | Risk of missed renewals and poor negotiating position | Medium | Introduce a central contract register with renewal reminders |
| Expenses | Expenses submitted through email | Process relies on manual review and document handling | Administrative burden and inconsistent audit trail | Medium | Standardise expense submission and approval process |
| Communication | Operational decisions occur across email and WhatsApp | Decisions can be difficult to trace | Reduced accountability and poor information continuity | Medium | Define which channels are used for operational decisions and approvals |
| Accounting | Xero is established as the core accounting platform | Core accounting system appears suitable for current needs | No immediate system replacement requirement | Low | Retain Xero and improve surrounding processes |
| Payroll | Payroll is outsourced | Specialist processing is already externalised | Limited operational concern identified at this stage | Low | Retain current model, subject to service review |
| Scalability | Current model relies heavily on manual processes and individual knowledge | Existing operating model may become harder to manage with a fifth site | Increased admin, control risk and management dependency | High | Standardise key processes before expansion |

---

## Initial Diagnostic Summary

The review indicates that Fictional & Co does not require a complete systems overhaul.

The main opportunity is to improve the way existing systems, processes and responsibilities work together.

The highest-priority areas are:

1. Purchasing and cost control
2. Supplier invoice management
3. Supplier data and commercial visibility
4. Management reporting
5. Labour cost visibility
6. Scalability of current operational processes

The current accounting platform and outsourced payroll model appear broadly fit for purpose.

The next phase will define the KPIs and management information required to improve financial and operational visibility.
