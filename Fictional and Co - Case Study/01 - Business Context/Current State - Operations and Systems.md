# Fictional & Co — Current State: Operations and Systems

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager  
**Business:** Fictional & Co  
**Location:** London  
**Sites:** 4  
**Annual Revenue:** £4.8m  
**Employees:** 65  
**Active Suppliers:** 45  

## Purpose

This document captures the current operating environment at Fictional & Co before any diagnostic assessment or recommendations are made.

The aim is to establish how finance, operations, reporting, purchasing, supplier management and systems currently work across the business.

---

## Accounting

Xero is the central accounting system.

Supplier invoices are entered into Xero by the Finance & Admin Assistant. Coding is reviewed by the Finance Manager before month-end.

---

## Sales

Each site operates through the same cloud-based POS provider.

Sales reports can be exported by venue, product category and trading day, but the information is not automatically connected to financial reporting.

---

## Purchasing

Site managers order directly from approved suppliers.

Some suppliers are used across several venues, while others are specific to individual sites.

There is no central purchase-order process.

---

## Supplier Invoices

Invoices arrive through a mixture of:

- Finance email
- Individual site email addresses
- Supplier portals

Site managers confirm whether goods or services were received before invoices are approved for payment.

---

## Supplier Management

A spreadsheet contains supplier names and basic contact information.

Payment terms, contract dates, agreed pricing and supplier ownership are not consistently recorded.

---

## Cash Flow

Finance maintains a rolling cash-flow forecast in Google Sheets.

Bank balances, payroll, supplier payments, VAT and other major payments are updated manually.

---

## Budgeting

An annual budget is created by site and consolidated for the group.

Actual performance is compared against budget monthly, primarily through spreadsheets exported from Xero.

---

## Management Reporting

Management receives a monthly:

- Profit & Loss statement
- Sales summary
- Management commentary

Creating the reporting pack requires exports from Xero, the POS system and several spreadsheets.

---

## Labour

Site managers prepare weekly rotas.

Payroll is outsourced.

Labour cost is reviewed mainly through monthly financial reporting rather than during the trading month.

---

## Contracts

Supplier, property and service contracts are stored in Google Drive.

A separate spreadsheet tracks some renewal dates, but it is not consistently maintained.

---

## Expenses

Managers submit expenses and receipts through email.

Finance reviews and records them.

---

## Communication

Operational communication happens through a combination of email and WhatsApp.

Decisions are sometimes difficult to trace later.

---

## Current Systems Overview

| Area | Current System / Method |
|---|---|
| Accounting | Xero |
| Sales / EPOS | Cloud-based POS |
| Payroll | External payroll provider |
| Budgeting | Excel / Google Sheets |
| Cash-flow forecasting | Google Sheets |
| Supplier invoices | Email + Xero |
| Expenses | Email / receipts |
| Documents | Google Drive |
| Communication | Email + WhatsApp |
| CRM / projects | Basic CRM |
| Contracts | Google Drive + spreadsheet |
| Reporting | Xero + POS + manual spreadsheets |

---

## Current-State Principle

This document records the existing environment only.

No conclusions, ratings or recommendations are made at this stage. The next phase of the case study will assess the current setup across finance, cost control, supplier management, reporting, processes, systems, controls and scalability.
