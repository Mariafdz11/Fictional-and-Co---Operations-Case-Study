# Fictional & Co — KPI Framework

**Project:** SME Operations & Finance Improvement Case Study  
**Role:** Operations Transformation Manager

## Purpose

This KPI framework defines the core financial and operational measures Fictional & Co should monitor to improve visibility, cost control and decision-making across its four sites.

The aim is not to track as many metrics as possible, but to focus on a small number of indicators that support timely management action.

---

## KPI Principles

The framework is designed around four principles:

1. **Relevant** — each KPI should support a real management decision.
2. **Timely** — operational KPIs should be reviewed frequently enough to allow action before month-end.
3. **Comparable** — measures should be consistent across sites.
4. **Actionable** — ownership and follow-up should be clear when performance moves outside expectations.

---

## Core KPI Framework

| KPI | Definition | Why It Matters | Frequency | Primary Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue vs Budget | Actual revenue compared with budget for the period | Shows whether each site is trading in line with expectations | Weekly / Monthly | Operations Manager / Finance Manager |
| Gross Margin % | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue | Measures how effectively sales convert into gross profit and highlights cost pressure | Weekly / Monthly | Finance Manager |
| Cost of Goods Sold % | Direct product cost as a percentage of revenue | Provides visibility of food and beverage cost performance | Weekly / Monthly | Site Managers / Operations Manager |
| Labour Cost % | Total labour cost as a percentage of revenue | Helps management control one of the largest operating costs | Weekly / Monthly | Site Managers / Operations Manager |
| Site Contribution | Revenue less direct site operating costs | Shows the operating contribution generated by each site before central overheads | Monthly | Finance Manager |
| Supplier Spend Variance | Change in supplier spend or unit pricing against prior period / agreed pricing | Identifies unusual increases and supports supplier cost control | Monthly | Finance Manager / Operations Manager |
| Budget vs Actual Operating Costs | Actual operating spend compared with budget by category | Highlights overspend early and supports accountability | Monthly | Finance Manager |
| 13-Week Cash Forecast | Expected cash inflows, outflows and closing cash position over 13 weeks | Provides forward visibility of liquidity and upcoming funding pressure | Weekly | Finance Manager |
| Accounts Payable Ageing | Outstanding supplier balances grouped by age | Helps manage payment obligations and supplier relationships | Weekly | Finance Team |
| Contract / Renewal Exposure | Value and timing of contracts approaching renewal | Supports negotiation, prevents missed renewals and improves cost control | Monthly | Operations Manager |

---

## Weekly Management View

The weekly operating review should focus on the KPIs that can still be influenced during the trading month.

### Weekly KPIs

- Revenue vs Budget
- Cost of Goods Sold %
- Labour Cost %
- 13-Week Cash Forecast
- Accounts Payable Ageing

The purpose of the weekly review is to identify exceptions early and agree actions before they become month-end problems.

---

## Monthly Management View

The monthly review should combine financial performance with operational trends.

### Monthly KPIs

- Revenue vs Budget
- Gross Margin %
- Cost of Goods Sold %
- Labour Cost %
- Site Contribution
- Supplier Spend Variance
- Budget vs Actual Operating Costs
- Contract / Renewal Exposure

The monthly review should also include commentary on significant movements, risks and agreed actions.

---

## KPI Definitions

### Revenue vs Budget

**Formula**

Revenue Variance = Actual Revenue - Budgeted Revenue

Revenue Variance % = (Actual Revenue - Budgeted Revenue) / Budgeted Revenue × 100

**Use**

Review by site and for the group overall.

---

### Gross Margin %

**Formula**

Gross Margin % = (Revenue - Cost of Goods Sold) / Revenue × 100

**Use**

Track margin movement by site and investigate significant deterioration.

---

### Cost of Goods Sold %

**Formula**

COGS % = Cost of Goods Sold / Revenue × 100

**Use**

Review by site and, where useful, by food and beverage category.

---

### Labour Cost %

**Formula**

Labour Cost % = Total Labour Cost / Revenue × 100

**Use**

Review weekly to allow rota adjustments before month-end.

---

### Site Contribution

**Formula**

Site Contribution = Revenue - Direct Site Costs

**Use**

Compare operating performance across sites without allocating central overheads that individual site managers cannot directly control.

---

### Supplier Spend Variance

**Formula**

Supplier Spend Variance = Current Period Spend - Comparison Period Spend

Where unit-level data is available, agreed or previous purchase prices should also be compared with current prices.

**Use**

Identify unusual increases, supplier concentration and opportunities for renegotiation.

---

### Budget vs Actual Operating Costs

**Formula**

Cost Variance = Actual Cost - Budgeted Cost

Cost Variance % = (Actual Cost - Budgeted Cost) / Budgeted Cost × 100

**Use**

Review material variances by cost category and assign follow-up actions.

---

### 13-Week Cash Forecast

**Measure**

Expected weekly opening cash, inflows, outflows and closing cash position for the next 13 weeks.

**Use**

Identify periods of cash pressure early enough to manage payment timing, working capital and management decisions.

---

### Accounts Payable Ageing

**Measure**

Outstanding supplier balances grouped into:

- Current
- 1–30 days overdue
- 31–60 days overdue
- 61+ days overdue

**Use**

Monitor upcoming obligations, overdue balances and supplier relationship risk.

---

### Contract / Renewal Exposure

**Measure**

Contracts due for renewal within the next:

- 30 days
- 60 days
- 90 days

Include annual value, owner and notice period where available.

**Use**

Create enough lead time to review supplier performance, renegotiate terms or exit contracts.

---

## Reporting Structure

The KPI dashboard should provide three levels of visibility:

### Group View

Overall performance across Fictional & Co.

### Site View

Soho, Vauxhall, Borough and Camden performance shown consistently.

### Exception View

Highlight only material variances or items requiring management action.

---

## Suggested Management Actions

KPIs should not be reported without follow-up.

Each material exception should record:

- Issue
- Site / area affected
- Owner
- Agreed action
- Target date
- Status

This turns the KPI dashboard from a reporting tool into a management tool.
